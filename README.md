Hey, I'm Bhavika 👋
I'm a Software Engineer (SDE II) working at the intersection of backend engineering, complex business workflows, and applied AI.

Professionally, I've built and shipped software for biotech and banking. My work involves turning complicated processes into reliable APIs, data models, interfaces, and workflows that people depend on. I enjoy the parts of engineering that require thinking beyond one service: integrations, orchestration, data correctness, failure recovery, and production debugging.

Independently, I build AI products from end to end. I care about what happens around the model: good retrieval, controlled agent workflows, grounded output, state, evaluation, observability, and a usable product experience.
I like systems that are easy to explain, hard to break, and useful beyond the demo.
